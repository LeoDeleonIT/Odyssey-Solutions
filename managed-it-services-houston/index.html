What managed IT support can include

  • Remote monitoring and management for covered computers and servers
  • Help desk support and documented escalation paths
  • Microsoft 365 and identity administration
  • Network, wireless, VPN, firewall, and vendor coordination
  • Patch management, endpoint protection, and access reviews
  • Backup monitoring and scheduled recovery testing
  • Technology lifecycle, budgeting, and project planning
Scope matters. Coverage, response targets, hours, third-party systems, and project work are defined in writing before service begins.

How managed IT onboarding works

1. Discover

We inventory critical systems, users, sites, vendors, recurring issues, and business risks. Existing documentation is validated instead of assumed.

2. Stabilize

Urgent access, backup, patching, security, and reliability gaps are prioritized according to likely operational impact.

3. Standardize

Devices, identities, support procedures, documentation, monitoring, and change controls are brought into a maintainable baseline.

4. Improve

Regular reviews connect technical work with business priorities, upcoming projects, lifecycle costs, and risk decisions.

Who this service fits
