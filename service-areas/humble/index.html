New dental office and expansion planning

Technology decisions are most affordable before construction and equipment orders are final. Odyssey can coordinate cabling, switching, firewall placement, secure wireless coverage, workstations, operatories, phones, printers, servers or cloud services, imaging connectivity, backups, power protection, and the opening-day test plan.

We document vendor responsibilities and dependencies early so the practice is less likely to discover missing ports, unsupported hardware, weak wireless coverage, or unclear conversion ownership during the final cutover.

Planning a new office, conversion, or equipment change? Bring the floor plan, vendor list, and target date. Odyssey will help identify technology dependencies and the next practical step. Book a consultation.

Software, imaging, analytics, and vendor environments

Odyssey supports the infrastructure and integrations surrounding Open Dental, Eaglesoft, imaging applications, intraoral sensors, Zocdoc, Practice by Numbers, MyDentalForce, EDS, Crown by Moolah, and selected Henry Schein products. Product licensing, proprietary database conversion, and application-specific support remain with the applicable vendor unless another responsibility is documented.

Our role is to prepare and maintain the environment, coordinate technical work, isolate problems across vendor boundaries, and test the complete workflow instead of treating each product as an unrelated island.

Cybersecurity and HIPAA-aligned safeguards

Dental cybersecurity should connect risk decisions to daily operations. Support can include named accounts, least privilege, multifactor authentication where supported, timely access removal, patching, endpoint safeguards, network segmentation, backup oversight, phishing defense, and incident planning.

No product or IT provider automatically makes a practice HIPAA compliant. Odyssey helps implement and document technical safeguards while the organization evaluates its administrative, physical, contractual, and legal responsibilities.
