## Review request process

Use a legitimate customer follow-up process after a completed engagement or a meaningful service milestone. Ask for an honest review, provide the official profile link, and do not offer rewards tied to a positive rating. Keep the request neutral. Do not ask employees, vendors, or people without direct customer experience to post reviews.

## Directory priorities

Prioritize the Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn company page, local chambers or business groups Odyssey actually belongs to, and relevant vendor or industry directories with real eligibility requirements. Keep the business name, phone, domain, service area, and description consistent. Do not create bulk directory submissions, spam backlinks, or promotional Reddit posts.
