Trinity Dental Centers: clearer ownership across locations

Odyssey took responsibility for coordinating a multi-location network environment after recurring outages and fragmented vendor ownership.

Published July 20, 2026Client-reported outcome

The situation

The operations lead reported working with two IT vendors while still experiencing outages almost every month. Multi-location environments magnify unclear ownership because connectivity, firewalls, internet providers, devices, and practice systems cross organizational boundaries.

The work

  • Consolidated responsibility for the covered network environment
  • Reviewed connectivity and security controls across locations
  • Established clearer monitoring, response, and escalation ownership
  • Connected technical support with the group’s operating priorities

The reported result

The client reported no unplanned outage for more than a year after the transition and described consistently fast response from Odyssey.

Context matters. Availability and response depend on scope, contracts, third-party carriers, system condition, and incident circumstances. This client experience is not a guarantee.

What multi-location practices can take from it

Inventorying site dependencies, standardizing network design, documenting escalation contacts, monitoring critical connections, and assigning one accountable coordinator can reduce the gaps that prolong outages.
