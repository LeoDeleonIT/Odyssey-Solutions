Cybersecurity for dental offices

Dental offices are a common target because they hold patient and payment data and often run older systems. A practical setup includes named logins for every staff member, multifactor sign-in for email and remote access, endpoint protection on every computer, a separate network for guests and devices, locked-down vendor remote access, email filtering, and staff who know what a phishing email looks like. See dental cybersecurity for the full list.

Backups and HIPAA safeguards

Your practice database and imaging need backups that are encrypted, kept off-site and restore-tested. See data backup and recovery for dental offices. HIPAA also expects a written security risk assessment; the HIPAA security risk assessment guide for Texas dental practices walks through it.

Is Odyssey a dental IT company near Cypress, TX?
