Security, backups, VPNs, and remote monitoring

Remote access and monitoring are valuable only when they are deliberately secured. We use approved tooling, named accounts, least privilege, multifactor authentication where supported, logging, patch management, and documented access removal. Network designs can separate clinical systems, guest traffic, connected devices, and administrative resources while preserving necessary vendor communication.

Backups are planned around recovery, not just successful jobs. Scope, retention, isolation, failure alerts, recovery priorities, and restore tests are documented so the practice knows what can be recovered and how long critical interruptions may last.

How getting started works

Most practices start with a short call about what is breaking, what is changing and which vendors are involved. From there the work usually follows four steps.

  1. Review the environment. Odyssey documents workstations, servers, the network, backups, accounts and vendor contacts, often through the Practice IT Readiness Review.
  2. Fix the urgent risks first. Failing backups, shared passwords, unsupported systems and single points of failure get a ranked plan before anything else.
  3. Take over day-to-day support. Staff get one place to ask for help, and Odyssey becomes the technical contact for software, imaging, phone and internet vendors.
  4. Plan ahead. Replacements, new operatories, new locations and software changes are scheduled around patient care instead of in the middle of it.

Dental IT support FAQ

What does a dental IT company do?

A dental IT company runs the technology a practice depends on every day: workstations, servers, the office network and Wi-Fi, Microsoft 365, imaging and sensor connectivity, backups, cybersecurity and the help desk. It also coordinates with practice-management, imaging, phone and internet vendors so the office has one technical owner when something breaks.

What dental technology does Odyssey Solutions support?

Odyssey supports the IT environment around Open Dental, Eaglesoft, imaging software, intraoral sensors, MyDentalForce, Practice by Numbers, Zocdoc, Crown by Moolah, EDS and selected Henry Schein products, plus networks, phones, printers and Microsoft 365. Application features, licenses and database conversions stay with each software vendor.

Can Odyssey help open a new dental office in Houston?

Yes. Odyssey plans cabling, network, Wi-Fi, workstations, operatory technology, imaging connectivity, phones, backups and vendor timing with the owner and contractor, then tests the workflows the team needs before opening day.

Do you offer on-site dental IT support near me?

Odyssey provides on-site support across Greater Houston, including Northwest Houston, Cypress, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Humble and Pearland. Practices outside the metro can use remote dental IT support when their systems allow it, with on-site travel quoted separately.

How is dental IT support priced?

Odyssey offers managed support as well as project-based and time-and-materials work. Cost depends on the number of locations, workstations and servers, the vendor systems in scope and how much after-hours coverage the practice needs. The dental office IT budget guide explains the main cost drivers.

Does dental IT support make a practice HIPAA compliant?

No. IT support can implement and document technical safeguards, but no IT provider or tool guarantees HIPAA compliance. The practice keeps responsibility for its risk analysis, policies, training and legal decisions.
