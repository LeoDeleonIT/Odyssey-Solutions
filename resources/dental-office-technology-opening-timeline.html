New Dental Office Technology Timeline: 90 Days to Opening

A phased technology plan gives the builder, carriers, software vendors, and practice team time to solve dependencies before opening day.

Published August 23, 20268-minute readDental IT guidePublished by Odyssey Solutions · Dental technology planning for Greater Houston.

New dental office technology projects involve dependencies that are easy to miss. Internet may depend on construction access, imaging may depend on vendor software, and phones may depend on the network design. A timeline should make those dependencies visible instead of leaving them for the final week.

Set the opening date, then work backward. Give every carrier, vendor, installer, and test a named owner and a date.

About 90 days out: map the project

  • Confirm the rooms, workflows, locations, equipment, and opening milestones.
  • List practice-management, imaging, phones, payments, internet, security, and backup vendors.
  • Confirm cabling routes, equipment locations, power, carrier handoff, and storage.
  • Document who approves changes and who will receive support after opening.

About 60 days out: order and configure

Order equipment with enough time for shipping and replacement. Confirm supported operating systems, ports, licensing, remote-access requirements, and user roles with each vendor. Configure the network, identity, endpoint protection, and backup plan in a controlled sequence.

About 30 days out: test the workflow

Test internet, Wi-Fi, phones, printing, scanning, imaging, practice software, payments, user access, and vendor support. Walk through the workflow from check-in to checkout. Test the rooms where the work will happen, not only the network closet.

Opening week: watch and document

Keep a short issue log, confirm support contacts, and schedule time for staff questions. Monitor the systems that affect patient flow and resolve small problems before they become recurring interruptions. Save the final equipment map, credentials ownership record, vendor list, and recovery instructions.

After opening: review the first month

Ask staff which steps feel slow, confusing, or fragile. Check backups, alerts, updates, permissions, and vendor access. Use the review to adjust support coverage and the next improvement list. Our dental office network setup guide covers the infrastructure details that support this timeline.

Planning note
