MyDentalForce for Multi-Location Dental Groups

A look at the operations MyDentalForce centralizes, its current Open Dental connection, and the technical preparation that supports a reliable rollout.

Published July 20, 202610-minute readReviewed against official product informationPublished by Odyssey Solutions · Dental technology and operations guidance for Texas groups.

Growing dental groups often reach a point where practice-management data exists but operating insight is scattered across locations, spreadsheets, messages, and manual follow-up. MyDentalForce positions itself as an operations platform for multi-location dental groups, built by a team that operates dental offices and designed to make performance and follow-through visible in one place.

Current integration note: MyDentalForce's official site advertises direct synchronization with Open Dental through a secure API. It says Dentrix integration is in development and Eaglesoft is on the roadmap, so groups should verify current availability before planning a rollout.

What MyDentalForce centralizes

According to its official product information, the platform brings together production and collection analytics, daily trends and goals, referral workflows, new-patient tracking, billing and claims oversight, reviews, role-based documents, bonuses, and custom reports. That creates an operations layer above the day-to-day practice-management workflow.

For leadership, the value is not another dashboard by itself. It is the ability to give regional leaders, office managers, and owners consistent definitions and a shared follow-up process across locations. A useful implementation should answer who owns each metric, how exceptions become work, and how the group validates the underlying data.

How the Open Dental connection changes planning

An API connection reduces manual exports, but it still requires disciplined preparation. Confirm the Open Dental versions and locations in scope, the exact data MyDentalForce will read or write, update frequency, identity and permission requirements, and how failures are detected. Record who opens a support case when numbers do not reconcile.

Before onboarding, clean up inactive users, location mappings, provider names, referral sources, fee schedules, and reporting definitions. Technology cannot resolve inconsistent operating definitions on its own. Establish a baseline report inside Open Dental so the group can compare totals and exceptions during validation.

Security and HIPAA questions to document

MyDentalForce states that data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and it signs Business Associate Agreements. Those are important vendor representations, but the dental group still needs to document its own implementation decisions and oversight.

  • Which roles can see organization-wide, location, financial, claims, referral, and document data?
  • How are users provisioned, changed, and removed when responsibilities change?
  • What administrator and access events are available for review?
  • Which systems are the source of truth, and how are corrections handled?
  • What is the incident-notification and escalation process?
  • How will the organization retain necessary operational records if a vendor relationship ends?

A multi-location rollout plan

  1. Define outcomes: choose a limited group of operating questions the platform must answer.
  2. Inventory the environment: document locations, databases, versions, networks, owners, and access.
  3. Normalize data: reconcile provider, location, referral, goal, and reporting definitions.
  4. Design roles: grant the minimum access each job needs and define approval owners.
  5. Pilot: validate one representative location or group before broad deployment.
  6. Reconcile: compare platform results against agreed source reports and investigate differences.
  7. Train for action: teach managers how a metric becomes a follow-up task, not just how to open a dashboard.
  8. Review: monitor adoption, exceptions, stale accounts, and integration health after launch.
