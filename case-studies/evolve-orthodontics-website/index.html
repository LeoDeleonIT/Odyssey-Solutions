Evolve Orthodontics: treating the website like infrastructure

Odyssey rebuilt the digital experience and coordinated the surrounding hosting, forms, and practice-technology touchpoints.

Published July 20, 2026Client-reported outcome

The situation

The practice needed more than a visual refresh. The website had to support patient action while fitting into the organization’s hosting, intake, and practice-system workflows.

The work

  • Rebuilt the website around clear patient journeys and services
  • Coordinated hosting and operational ownership
  • Connected intake workflows with the practice’s approved systems
  • Treated performance, security, and maintenance as ongoing infrastructure concerns

The reported result

The practice owner reported a noticeable increase in new-patient inquiries and valued Odyssey’s approach to the website as operational infrastructure rather than an isolated marketing project.

Privacy boundary: Website forms and third-party services must be evaluated before collecting sensitive information. A vendor or hosting label alone does not make every workflow HIPAA compliant.

What other practices can take from it

Successful healthcare websites connect content, accessibility, performance, forms, scheduling, analytics, hosting, security, and internal follow-up. Each integration needs a clear owner and an intentional data path.
