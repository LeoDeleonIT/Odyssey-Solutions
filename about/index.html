A clear point of contact from day one

Know who you work with and what Odyssey is responsible for

Odyssey helps Houston businesses manage computers, networks, cloud services, vendors, and security work. We also have hands-on experience in dental and healthcare settings.

Technology becomes difficult when ownership is fragmented. The network vendor points to the software vendor, the software vendor points to the workstation, and the person responsible for the business is left coordinating the response. Odyssey was built to close those gaps with a named point of contact and documented responsibilities.

Accountability is defined before delivery. Before work begins, the proposal states what Odyssey will handle, what the client must provide or decide, and what stays with other vendors. That written division of responsibility keeps the work accountable.

From consultation to delivery

1. Understand the operation

Odyssey starts with the people, workflows, systems, recurring problems, upcoming changes, and consequences of downtime. If we are not the right fit, we will say so before any agreement begins.

2. Map ownership and dependencies

Odyssey documents the relevant users, devices, cloud services, networks, software vendors, security controls, backups, contracts, and escalation paths instead of treating each symptom as an isolated ticket.

3. Agree on scope and ownership

We recommend a next step based on the cost of downtime, the risk involved, what your team can maintain, and what Odyssey can own.

4. Keep delivery accountable

During delivery, Odyssey coordinates the work inside its scope, tracks outside vendor dependencies, documents important decisions, and flags anything that still needs an owner.
