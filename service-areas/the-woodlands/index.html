Offices in The Woodlands run from small firms in Hughes Landing and Town Center to medical practices off Research Forest Drive. Most are too small for a full-time IT person but too busy to have the office manager troubleshooting the network. Odyssey is the outside IT team for that size of business: one number to call, one team that owns the problem, and the same people who know your setup each time.

IT help desk services

Your staff call or open a ticket, and a technician connects remotely, usually the same day. Most problems are passwords, email, printers, slow computers, shared files and software errors, and most get fixed in one session. If it needs hands on hardware, we schedule a visit.

  • Help desk by phone and remote session for every employee
  • Workstation and laptop setup, patching and replacement planning
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace: email, accounts, multifactor sign-in, shared files and access changes
  • Firewalls, Wi-Fi, switches and the internet provider
  • Endpoint protection, monitoring and alerts
  • Backups that are checked and restore-tested
  • Printers, phones and the vendors behind your line-of-business software

Cloud services and Microsoft 365

A lot of Woodlands offices still run on an old server in a closet, or a cloud setup someone half-finished. We can move files to SharePoint and OneDrive or Google Drive, set up email properly, turn on multifactor sign-in, clean up who has access to what, and make sure the cloud data is backed up too, since Microsoft 365 alone is not a full backup. See Microsoft 365 support for small businesses.

Outsourced IT or an in-house hire?

If you have fewer than about 50 people, one in-house IT person usually ends up stretched across help desk, security, vendors and projects with no backup when they are out. Outsourced IT gives you a team for a predictable monthly cost. Larger companies sometimes keep an internal IT lead and use Odyssey for help desk overflow, security and projects. For pricing ranges, read what managed IT costs in Houston.

Security and backups

Support includes endpoint protection, patching, multifactor sign-in, email filtering, removing access when someone leaves, and backups that are tested by actually restoring them. If you handle patient or financial records, we document the safeguards so you can show them to an auditor or insurer.

Medical and dental offices

Practices near the hospitals and along Research Forest and Woodlands Parkway get the same help desk and security work, plus support for practice software, imaging and the vendors around them. See dental IT support and healthcare IT support.

Frequently asked questions

Does Odyssey provide on-site IT support in The Woodlands?

Yes. Most help desk requests are handled remotely, and Odyssey schedules on-site visits in The Woodlands and south Montgomery County for hardware, network and setup work.

What does an IT help desk service include?

Phone and remote support for your staff with passwords, email, printers, computers, shared files and software problems, plus monitoring, patching and someone who escalates to vendors for you.

Can you move our office to the cloud?

Yes. Odyssey can move files and email to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, set up access and multifactor sign-in, and back up the cloud data.
