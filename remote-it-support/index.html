Frequently asked questions

What to know before remote support begins

Can Odyssey support a business outside Texas?

Potentially. Remote engagements are evaluated according to the systems involved, support expectations, security requirements, time zone, and availability of qualified local help when physical work is necessary.

What can remote IT support include?

Remote support can include user help, workstation troubleshooting, Microsoft 365 administration, account and access changes, endpoint management, security coordination, backup oversight, onboarding, offboarding, and vendor troubleshooting within the agreed scope.

Does Odyssey provide 24-hour support?

Standard business hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central Time. Coverage, response expectations, and contracted terms are documented before service begins.

What problems require someone on-site?

Failed hardware, cabling, physical installations, some network changes, and other hands-on work may require a qualified local technician. Odyssey identifies those dependencies and coordinates the next practical step within the agreed scope.

Can Odyssey support a HIPAA-regulated organization remotely?

Odyssey can support technical safeguards, secure access, workforce processes, and documentation for healthcare environments. Remote IT support does not by itself make an organization HIPAA compliant.
