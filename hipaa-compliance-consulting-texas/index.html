Important: Odyssey provides technology and operational guidance, not legal advice. No consultant, software, badge, or checklist can guarantee compliance. Each regulated organization remains responsible for evaluating its obligations with appropriate legal and compliance professionals.

What a useful risk analysis looks like

A risk analysis should identify where electronic protected health information is created, received, maintained, and transmitted; assess realistic threats and vulnerabilities; document current controls; and support decisions about additional safeguards. It must reflect the organization’s real environment.

Evidence matters

Policies should match practice. Training logs, access approvals, recovery-test results, risk decisions, vendor records, and remediation updates help show that the program is active and maintained.

Designed for dental operations

Dental environments combine practice-management software, imaging, sensors, online scheduling, claims, payments, email, cloud tools, and outside vendors. Our work connects compliance responsibilities with the technology dependencies your team uses every day.
