How we publish

Editorial and corrections policy

Odyssey Solutions publishes practical technology guidance for people responsible for dental, healthcare, security, compliance, and business operations.

Last updated July 20, 2026

Purpose and audience

Our resources are designed to help readers ask better questions, understand dependencies, and make more informed operational decisions. Content is educational and does not replace legal, regulatory, financial, clinical, or vendor-specific advice.

Research standard

We prioritize primary and official sources, including government agencies, standards organizations, and vendor documentation. Where a statement depends on product version, contract terms, jurisdiction, or organizational circumstances, readers are told to verify those details.

Authorship and review

Articles identify the responsible Odyssey Solutions team and link to author or reviewer information. A human reviewer is responsible for the published scope, factual checks, claims, links, and vendor boundaries.

AI-assisted work

Odyssey may use AI-assisted drafting, organization, editing, or quality checks. AI output is not published as an authority. A human reviewer remains responsible for verifying sources, removing unsupported statements, protecting confidential information, and approving publication.

Vendor relationships

References to software, hardware, and service companies describe experience, interoperability, or educational context unless a formal relationship is explicitly disclosed. Product names belong to their respective owners. Mention does not imply endorsement, certification, authorization, or partnership.

Compliance and security claims

We do not claim that a product or technology provider alone guarantees HIPAA compliance, cybersecurity, breach prevention, or a legal outcome. Recommendations must be evaluated against the organization’s actual environment and responsibilities.

Updates and corrections

Material updates should revise the visible date. Errors are corrected as soon as reasonably practical. Readers can report a concern to leo@odysseysolutions.co with the page URL and supporting detail.

Client stories

Named testimonials and case studies are published from approved client material. Results are context-specific and are not promises of future performance. We label outcomes as client-reported when based on a client statement.
