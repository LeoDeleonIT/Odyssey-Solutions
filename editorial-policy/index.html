How we publish

Editorial and corrections policy

Odyssey Solutions publishes technology guidance for people responsible for dental, healthcare, security, compliance, and business operations.

Last updated August 13, 2026

Purpose and audience

Our articles explain what to check, which systems or vendors may be involved, and when to ask a specialist. They are educational and do not replace legal, regulatory, financial, clinical, or vendor-specific advice.

Research standard

We prioritize primary and official sources, including government agencies, standards organizations, and vendor documentation. Where a statement depends on product version, contract terms, jurisdiction, or organizational circumstances, readers are told to verify those details.

Authorship and review

Each article identifies its named author or Odyssey Solutions as the publisher. Before publication, Odyssey reviews the scope, sources, factual claims, links, and vendor boundaries.

AI-assisted work

Odyssey may use AI to organize notes, draft sections, or check copy. We do not treat AI output as a source. An Odyssey reviewer verifies sources and claims, removes unsupported statements, protects confidential information, and approves publication.

Vendor relationships

We mention products when relevant to a workflow, compatibility issue, or example. Unless explicitly stated, a mention does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, authorization, or partnership. Product names belong to their respective owners.

Compliance and security claims

We do not claim that a product or technology provider alone guarantees HIPAA compliance, cybersecurity, breach prevention, or a legal outcome. Recommendations must be evaluated against the organization’s actual environment and responsibilities.

Updates and corrections

When we make a significant change, we update the date shown on the article. We correct errors as soon as reasonably practical. Readers can report a concern to info@odysseysolutions.co with the page URL and supporting detail.

Client stories

We publish a client’s name or testimonial only with permission. When a result comes from the client, we label it client-reported. Results vary and are not promises of future performance.
