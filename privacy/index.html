Privacy policy

Effective August 13, 2026

This policy explains how Odyssey Solutions handles information submitted through odysseysolutions.co. It does not describe client-service data governed by a separate contract.

Information we receive

We may receive contact details, organization information, service interests, and the message you submit. Basic technical logs may include IP address, browser, device, referral, and page activity. During a browsing session, the site may retain the initial landing page and campaign tags so they can accompany a contact-form submission.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, schedule conversations, understand how someone found Odyssey, provide requested services, secure and improve the website, measure performance, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

The contact form uses Formspree. Scheduling links open Calendly only when selected and may include limited source, campaign, and page context. They do not include contact-form entries or sensitive information. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Hosting, email, security, and analytics providers may process limited information to operate the site.

Sensitive information

Do not submit protected health information, passwords, payment-card data, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through the public website or scheduling form.

Retention and sharing

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, security, records, and legal obligations. Odyssey does not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers, with consent, to protect rights and security, or when legally required.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of contact information, subject to applicable exceptions. Email info@odysseysolutions.co.

Policy changes

Material changes will update the effective date on this page. Questions can be directed to the same email address.
