We prioritize technology according to its effect on care, privacy, revenue, communication, and recovery.

01

Users, access, and help desk

Support staff, manage accounts, document approvals, apply least privilege, coordinate onboarding and offboarding, and keep recurring issues from becoming accepted downtime.

02

05

06

Map application dependencies, supported configurations, interfaces, contacts, data flows, business associate relationships, escalation paths, and each provider's responsibility.

Common questions

Healthcare IT support FAQ

What does a healthcare IT support company manage?

A provider can coordinate help desk support, user access, devices, Microsoft 365, networks, cybersecurity, backups, documentation, and technical work with EHR and other healthcare vendors. Exact scope should be written into the service agreement.

Does using a healthcare IT provider make us HIPAA compliant?

No. IT support can strengthen and document safeguards, but no vendor or product guarantees compliance. The regulated organization retains broader risk analysis, policy, workforce, physical, and legal responsibilities.

Can Odyssey work with our existing EHR vendor?

Yes. Odyssey can manage surrounding infrastructure and document dependencies, supported configurations, contacts, and escalation paths while the EHR vendor remains responsible for its application.

Is on-site support available in Houston?

Yes. Odyssey provides on-site support across Greater Houston and remote support for compatible environments. Coverage and response expectations are confirmed during discovery.
