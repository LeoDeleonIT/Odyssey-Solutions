How coverage works

The right support method for the problem

Many incidents can be diagnosed and resolved remotely. Physical installations, cabling, device failures, office openings, and some network work require hands on-site.

  • Remote support: help desk, monitoring, Microsoft 365, identity, security tooling, vendor coordination, and many software issues
  • On-site support: imaging and sensor troubleshooting, network hardware, workstations, servers, cabling coordination, and office buildouts
  • Planned projects: conversions, moves, new locations, equipment refreshes, VPNs, and multi-site standardization
  • Clear boundaries: coverage, hours, travel, third-party systems, and response expectations are documented before work begins

Outside the Houston metro?

Remote support may still be a fit when the work can be performed securely without a local visit. Odyssey can support cloud administration, managed endpoints, documentation, security coordination, vendors, and eligible line-of-business applications from a distance.

Organizations that need recurring hands-on coverage outside Greater Houston may require a local field partner. We confirm that during discovery instead of overpromising.
