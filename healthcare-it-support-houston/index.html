Discuss healthcare IT

Healthcare IT must balance availability, security, usability, and regulatory responsibilities. Odyssey builds support around the clinical and business processes technology enables.

What we support

  • Workstations, laptops, shared clinical devices, and lifecycle planning
  • Microsoft 365, email, identities, access, and multifactor authentication
  • Networks, secure wireless, firewalls, VPNs, and multiple locations
  • Endpoint monitoring, patching, security, and help desk support
  • Backup oversight, recovery testing, and contingency planning
  • Line-of-business application and vendor coordination
  • Onboarding, role changes, termination, and access evidence

Support aligned with risk

Systems are prioritized by their effect on patient care, operations, privacy, and recovery. The result is a clearer roadmap than treating every technical issue as equally urgent.

HIPAA-aligned safeguards

Odyssey can help implement and document technical safeguards and operational practices that support a healthcare organization’s security program. This work does not guarantee compliance or replace legal advice, organization-wide risk analysis, or administrative and physical safeguards.

Vendor coordination

Healthcare organizations depend on EHR, imaging, communications, billing, scheduling, laboratory, and other vendors. We document support boundaries, dependencies, contacts, and escalation procedures so issues are less likely to stall between providers.
