On-site Spring visitsRemote help deskDental and medical offices

Spring practices tend to sit in strip centers and medical office buildings along FM 1960, Louetta, Kuykendahl, FM 2920 and the I-45 corridor. That usually means shared building wiring, a network closet you did not design, and an internet circuit someone else ordered. When the schedule, the phones or the x-ray software stops working, the office needs one person to figure out which piece broke and who owns it. That is the job Odyssey does.

What day-to-day support covers

  • Help desk for front-office, clinical and admin staff, by phone or remote session
  • Practice-management software environments such as Open Dental and Eaglesoft, plus vendor coordination
  • Imaging software, intraoral sensors, shared storage and the network paths between operatories
  • Patching, endpoint protection and remote monitoring for every workstation and server
  • Microsoft 365, email, multifactor sign-in and access changes when staff join or leave
  • Backups that are checked, alerted on and restore-tested, not just scheduled
  • Firewalls, Wi-Fi, internet providers and phone vendors handled in one place
Remote first, on-site when it counts. Password resets, software errors, cloud issues and monitoring alerts are usually fixed remotely the same day. Dead hardware, cabling, sensors and office moves get a scheduled visit.

Storm season and power problems

North Harris County gets its share of storms and outages. A power blip that reboots the server in the middle of the day, or a lightning surge that takes out a switch, is a common reason a Spring office calls for help. Odyssey plans battery backup for the server, network gear and phones, checks that equipment shuts down cleanly when the battery runs low, and keeps a copy of your data off-site so a bad day at the office does not become a lost day of records.

Opening or moving an office in Spring

A lot of new practices in Spring open in second-generation space or new construction near the Grand Parkway. Bring IT in before the build-out drawings are final. Odyssey can plan cabling drops for each operatory and the front desk, the network closet, the firewall and Wi-Fi coverage, phones, printers, imaging connections and the internet order, then test everything before the first patient.

Have a floor plan and an opening date? Send them over with your vendor list. We will tell you what has to happen first. Book a consultation.

The new dental office IT checklist walks through the same steps if you want to plan ahead on your own.

Security and HIPAA safeguards

Support can include named logins for every staff member, multifactor sign-in where the software allows it, removing access the day someone leaves, patching, endpoint protection, a separate guest Wi-Fi, phishing defense and a written plan for what to do if something goes wrong.

No IT provider can make a practice HIPAA compliant on its own. Odyssey puts the technical safeguards in place and documents them, while the practice handles its policies, training and agreements. If you need help with those, see HIPAA guidance and HIPAA training.
