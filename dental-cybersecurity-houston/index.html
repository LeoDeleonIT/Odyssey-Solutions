  • Identity, privilege, multifactor authentication, and account lifecycle reviews

Security for dental technology

Practice-management systems, imaging software, intraoral sensors, payment workflows, analytics, and online scheduling all create dependencies. Odyssey helps define how these systems connect and who owns the infrastructure, access, backup, and escalation surrounding them.

No product guarantees security or HIPAA compliance. Effective risk management combines administrative, physical, and technical safeguards with informed organizational decisions.

A risk-informed approach

Identify what matters

Critical systems, sensitive data, privileged access, vendors, and likely disruption paths are mapped before controls are prioritized.

Reduce realistic exposure

We prioritize gaps by their likely operational, privacy, and recovery impact. Generic fear-based checklists do not set the order.

Test the response

Backups, escalation procedures, contact information, and decision roles need periodic testing. Documentation that cannot be used during an incident is not enough.

Cybersecurity and HIPAA

Technical safeguards can support a regulated organization’s security program, but compliance depends on the organization’s full circumstances and responsibilities. See our HIPAA compliance guidance and dental HIPAA checklist.
