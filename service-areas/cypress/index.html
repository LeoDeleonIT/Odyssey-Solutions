Cypress practices opening, expanding, or modernizing an office need technology decisions made early. Internet delivery, cabling, network closets, equipment placement, imaging, software, phones, wireless coverage, security, and vendor timelines all affect the final cutover.

New dental office and expansion support

  • Technology requirements and responsibility mapping before construction advances
  • Cabling and network design coordination with contractors and equipment vendors
  • Secure wired and wireless networks, firewalls, VPNs, and segmented access
  • Workstations, servers, operatories, imaging software, and intraoral sensors
  • Practice-management software, cloud services, phones, payments, and analytics
  • Testing, documentation, cutover planning, and post-opening stabilization

Ongoing IT after opening day

A successful opening is only the beginning. Remote monitoring, patching, endpoint protection, backups, access management, help desk support, and vendor coordination keep the environment maintainable after the project team leaves.

Reduce change-day surprises. Confirm who owns software configuration, imaging compatibility, data conversion, hardware installation, internet activation, DNS, phones, payments, and final testing. Odyssey can maintain a shared responsibility list.

When on-site support matters

Physical hardware, cabling, wireless surveys, imaging devices, and installation work may require a scheduled visit in Cypress. User support, monitoring, cloud administration, many software issues, and vendor calls can often be handled remotely. Exact scope and availability are confirmed before work begins.

Build from a documented standard

For multi-location organizations, a standard approach to networks, device naming, security controls, backups, documentation, and vendors makes new sites easier to support. Read the Open Dental conversion checklist when a software change is part of the project.
