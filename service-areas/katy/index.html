IT support and dental IT in Katy, Texas

Support for Katy practices and growing businesses, from the front desk and operatories to the network, backups and the next location you open.

Katy keeps growing west along I-10, the Westpark Tollway and the Grand Parkway, and the city itself crosses Harris, Fort Bend and Waller counties. For a practice or small business that usually means one of two things: you are opening in a brand-new building, or you are adding a second location and need both offices to work like one. Odyssey handles the day-to-day support and the planning for both.

What day-to-day support covers

  • Help desk for front-office, clinical and admin staff, by phone or remote session
  • Open Dental, Eaglesoft, imaging software and intraoral sensor environments, plus vendor coordination
  • Workstations, servers, secure Wi-Fi, firewalls, VPNs and internet providers
  • Patching, endpoint protection, remote monitoring and backup checks
  • Microsoft 365, email, multifactor sign-in and access changes when staff join or leave
Remote first, on-site when it counts. Most software, account and monitoring issues are fixed remotely. Failed hardware, cabling, imaging equipment and installs get a scheduled visit in Katy.

New construction and first-time build-outs

A lot of Katy offices open in new retail and medical centers in Cinco Ranch, along the Grand Parkway and out toward Fulshear. New buildings are a chance to get the technology right the first time, but only if IT is involved before the walls close. Odyssey plans cabling for each room and operatory, the network closet, power for the rack, firewall and Wi-Fi placement, phones, printers, imaging connections and the internet order, which can take weeks to install in a new development. Then we test it all before opening day.

Have a floor plan and an opening date? Send them over with your vendor list and we will tell you what has to happen first. Book a consultation.

Adding a second location

When a Katy practice adds a location, the hard part is making both offices share schedules, imaging, phones and files without slowing either one down. Odyssey sets up a site-to-site connection or cloud setup that fits your software, standard workstation builds so every room works the same way, shared logins and access rules, and one backup and monitoring plan that covers both buildings.

Security and HIPAA safeguards

Support can include named logins, multifactor sign-in where the software allows it, removing access the day someone leaves, patching, endpoint protection, a separate guest Wi-Fi, phishing defense, tested backups and a written incident plan your staff can follow.

No IT provider can make a practice HIPAA compliant on its own. Odyssey puts the technical safeguards in place and documents them, while the practice handles its policies, training and agreements.

Frequently asked questions

Does Odyssey provide on-site IT support in Katy, TX?

Yes. Odyssey schedules on-site visits in Katy and west Houston and handles most day-to-day requests remotely. Coverage and response times are confirmed before service starts.

Can Odyssey set up IT for a new office in Katy?

Yes. Odyssey can plan and coordinate cabling, the network closet, firewall, Wi-Fi, workstations, operatories, imaging connections, phones, internet service and opening-day testing.

Can you connect two practice locations?

Yes, when the software and scope fit. Multi-site work covers secure connections between offices, shared logins and access, standard workstation builds, and one backup and monitoring plan.
