Sugar Land has a lot of small offices doing serious work: medical and dental practices, accounting and law firms, and professional services along US-59, Highway 6 and around Town Square and First Colony. They need IT that is secure and reliable but cannot justify a full-time IT department. Odyssey fills that role.

What IT support covers

  • Help desk by phone and remote session for every employee
  • Workstation and laptop setup, patching and replacement planning
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace: email, accounts, multifactor sign-in, shared files and access changes
  • Firewalls, Wi-Fi, switches and the internet provider
  • Endpoint protection, monitoring and alerts
  • Backups that are checked and restore-tested
  • Printers, phones and the vendors behind your line-of-business software
Remote first, on-site when it counts. Most requests are fixed remotely the same day. Hardware, cabling and installs get a scheduled visit in Sugar Land.

Cloud services

We set up and clean up Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace: email, shared files, Teams, access rules and multifactor sign-in. If your files still live on an aging server, we plan the move to the cloud or a replacement, and we make sure the cloud data is backed up, not just synced.

For firms that handle sensitive records

Accounting, legal, medical and dental offices carry records that attackers want and regulators care about. Support can include email filtering, endpoint protection, encrypted laptops, separate admin accounts, quick access removal when someone leaves, and a written record of the safeguards for insurers or auditors. Tax season and patient hours are when downtime hurts most, so updates and changes are scheduled around them.

Storms, flooding and backups

Fort Bend has seen its share of flooding and power loss. Backups need a copy away from the office, battery backup should let the server shut down cleanly, and you should know how to keep working from home if the office is closed. We test the restore so you know it works before you need it.
