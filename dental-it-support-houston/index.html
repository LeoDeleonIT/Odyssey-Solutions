Dental software, imaging, sensors, and integrations

Odyssey supports the IT environment around dental software and technology, including Open Dental setup and conversion coordination, Eaglesoft environments, imaging software, intraoral sensors, MyDentalForce, Practice by Numbers, Zocdoc, Crown by Moolah, EDS, and selected Henry Schein products. Product capabilities, licenses, database conversion, and application-specific support remain with each vendor; our role is to prepare and operate the infrastructure, coordinate technical work, and verify the complete workflow.

Security, backups, VPNs, and remote monitoring

Remote access and monitoring are valuable only when they are deliberately secured. We use approved tooling, named accounts, least privilege, multifactor authentication where supported, logging, patch management, and documented access removal. Network designs can separate clinical systems, guest traffic, connected devices, and administrative resources while preserving necessary vendor communication.

Backups are planned around recovery, not just successful jobs. Scope, retention, isolation, failure alerts, recovery priorities, and restore tests are documented so the practice knows what can be recovered and how long critical interruptions may last.

Why practices choose a dental-focused IT partner

Generic technical support may fix a computer without understanding the appointment, imaging, claims, or patient impact around it. A dental-focused provider maps support priority to the practice workflow, maintains vendor contacts and environment documentation, and plans change windows around clinical operations.
